01 / Status vocabulary

Three labels that must not be mixed up

Blocked

A test outcome

A harmless local fixture was stopped by a deterministic harness rule. It says this specific synthetic test stopped.

It does not prove a matching production defense exists for every card.

Enforced

A connected control

A tested CanaryNorth policy control is connected to the named paired path and automatically makes the decision.

It does not mean enterprise deployment or universal coverage.

Shadow-only

A watch-and-report rehearsal

The lab recognizes the synthetic scenario and creates reviewable evidence, but it does not automatically stop anything.

It is a planned defense requirement, not live prevention.

04 / Evidence, ledger, and receipts

Evidence means “show your work”

Evidence is the safe supporting record used to explain a decision. A receipt records what was requested, the decision, why it happened, and what explicitly did not happen. Raw hostile content is withheld, while hashes and redacted metadata preserve correlation.
